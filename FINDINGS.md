# Findings: Retaining Wall Cost Register (US, 2026)

All figures are as published; no figures were computed except ratios noted as such. Version 2026-10-21.

- **National averages agree on project totals, not on per-square-foot.** Angi's average project is $6,081 (2,556 projects; typical $3,190–$9,202) and LawnStarter's is $6,300 ($3,500–$9,400). Per sq ft, LawnStarter says $20–$53 (avg $33) while HomeGuide says $35–$65.
- **Regional contractor prices span roughly 3x for segmental block.** Atlanta Retaining Wall Co. lists 4–6 ft block walls starting at $30–$40/sq ft; Schifsky Companies (Twin Cities) lists segmental block at $85–$120/sq ft. East Bay CA (Elite Development Builders): $25–$75/sq ft across types.
- **Height drives per-foot price.** cpaver.com (coastal SC) published three completed projects: $80/LF (2-ft wall), $284/LF (3.5-ft), $535/LF (6-ft engineered). Its own recent projects run $150–$550/LF, versus $35–$200+/LF it attributes to low-end market quotes.
- **Engineering/permit adders for taller walls:** $500–$2,000 (Atlanta), $1,500–$5,000 engineered design (coastal SC), $2,500–$5,000 (Twin Cities, walls over 4 ft).
- **Repair:** HomeGuide $100–$1,500 per repair; Angi typical $700 ($450–$950). Rebuild: HomeGuide $6,000–$14,000.
- **Contractor minimums:** HomeGuide reports $1,500–$3,000 project minimums.
